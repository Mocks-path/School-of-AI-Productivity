Which PM tasks are safe to automate?

Deciding whether to build is one thing; deciding whether it's safe to let a machine do unattended is another. Score each task on frequency (how often), pain (how tedious), and stability (does it stay the same) — but also on stakes: anything client-facing needs a human in the loop, never full auto-send. Make the expert call on each real PM task.
