Vet the brief — spot the fabricated citations

AI models are fluent, which means they can invent a citation that looks flawless: a real-sounding journal, a plausible author, a valid-format DOI — for a paper that never existed. An AI brief handed you five references. Two are fabricated. Use the tests — does the DOI resolve, does the title/author/journal actually match, does the content say what's claimed? — to judge each as real or fake.

4

Should you disclose the AI use here?

Disclosure isn't about guilt — it's about honesty and following the rules that apply. The honest test: did AI shape the substance (ideas, analysis, wording that ships), does a policy or instructor require it, and would a reader feel misled if they knew? If yes to any, disclose — clearly and specifically. Make the call on each real scenario.
