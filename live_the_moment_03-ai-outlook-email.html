AI in Outlook — from a full inbox to a clear next action

Email eats hours because most of it is reading, deciding and re-typing. AI in Outlook can summarise a 40-message thread, draft the reply, and turn "let's meet" into a calendar hold — but only if you drive it. Four hands-on drills: run the email workflow, pick the reply that actually fits the moment, triage a live inbox, and catch AI when it invents a commitment or picks the wrong recipient.

The email workflow — five moves, every message

Copilot in Outlook can "Summarise" a thread and "Draft with Copilot" a reply in seconds. That speed is a trap if you skip straight to Send. A message you're accountable for follows an order: understand what's being asked, decide your answer, let AI draft it, read every line, then send. The AI writes fast; the judgement and the accountability stay yours. Skip the review step and you'll agree to a deadline you never meant to commit to.

STEP 1
Summarise
Have Copilot condense the thread into what's being asked, who's involved and what's still open. A long thread hides the actual question.
→
STEP 2
Decide
You choose the answer — yes, no, "not yet," a date, a hand-off. This is your call, not the AI's. It can't decide what you're willing to commit to.
→
STEP 3
Draft
Tell Copilot your decision plus the tone and length you want. "Draft a short, warm reply declining, offering next month." Give it the point to make.
→
STEP 4
Review
Read the whole draft. Check every fact, name, date and promise. AI invents details that "sound right." This is the step people skip and regret.
→
STEP 5
Send
Confirm the recipients (To vs Reply-All), then send — or turn it into a task or meeting instead. Once it's out, it's out.

Triage the inbox — sort each message to its action

Triage is deciding, fast, what each email is before you touch it. Copilot can summarise and even suggest a category, but you own the sort. Every message resolves to one of four actions: Reply now (needs a real answer from you), Turn into a task (an action for later, no reply needed yet), Turn into a meeting (it can't be settled in writing), or Archive / ignore (FYI, newsletter, nothing owed). Drag each of the six messages to where it belongs. Wrong drops turn red — hover them to learn the fix.

✓

The 20-second check before you hit Send

Before any AI-drafted email leaves your outbox, run it past these four. Most embarrassing email mistakes are caught in under a minute — if you actually read the draft instead of trusting it.

  • 👤
    Right recipients? Check To, Cc and especially Reply-All. AI drops your draft into the thread as-is — it won't notice you're about to send a private "no" to forty people.
  • 📌
    Did you actually commit to that? AI loves to add "I'll have it to you by Friday." Read every promise, date and number. If you didn't decide it, delete it.
  • ✎
    Are the facts real? Names, figures, order numbers and "as we discussed" claims can be hallucinated. If the draft states something you can't personally confirm, cut it or verify it.
  • ♨
    Does the tone fit? AI defaults to breezy and long. For a complaint, a boss, or bad news, reread it as the recipient. Warm where it needs warmth, short where they're busy.
