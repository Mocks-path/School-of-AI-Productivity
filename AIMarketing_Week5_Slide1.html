Concept 2 of 4

The List — Quality Beats Size

Beginners chase a big list. Professionals build an engaged one. Email is permission-based: someone chose to let you into their inbox, and that choice is the entire value. A thousand people who genuinely want to hear from you will out-earn ten thousand who forgot they signed up.

Grow it the honest way — a clear opt-in, a lead magnet worth an email, a promise you keep. And the rule with no exceptions: never buy a list.

Content & Personalization

The Honest Slide

Permission, Trust & the Limits of AI

The slide most email courses carefully avoid. Three hard truths before you send anything.

Permission is everything

A small engaged list beats a big cold one every time — and you never, ever buy a list. Email runs on consent and trust, full stop.

The metrics are your honesty check

If people mark you as spam or unsubscribe, no clever subject line fixes that — the message or the frequency is wrong, and the numbers are telling you so.

Trust opens more than clicks

Apple's Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens by pre-loading images. Treat opens as a rough signal and trust clicks and conversions more.

AI is leverage, not magic. It can't guarantee the inbox and it can't manufacture trust you haven't earned. Send things people are genuinely glad to receive — then let AI help you send them well.
