Should you automate this — or just do it?

Automation is not free: it takes time to build and time to maintain. The honest test has three levers — frequency (how often), pain (how tedious/error-prone), and stability (does the layout stay the same). High on all three = automate. Low on any = do it by hand and move on. Make the expert call on each real task.
