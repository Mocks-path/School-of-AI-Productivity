Chapter Three · AI On-Page & Content Optimization

Draft & Optimize the Page — AI Writes, You Add Real Experience

Google (and readers) give a page seconds to prove it's worth ranking. AI can draft your guide in minutes — but generic prompts produce thin "AI slop" that helps no one. Your job is direction and first-hand experience: the E-E-A-T Google rewards.

📖 The story so far Wednesday. You asked Claude for a "how to use a moka pot" draft; it produced a competent-but-bland 900 words that read like every other guide. So you did the real work: you added the roaster's actual tips (the one about pulling it off the heat early), a photo of your own moka pot, and honest fixes for common mistakes. The same topic can read like slop or like a trusted expert — depending on the voice and experience you bring. Try all three angles below.

Your on-page optimization checklist

  • Write a title tag & meta description with the keyword (under 60 / 155 chars)
  • Structure one H1 + logical H2/H3 headings around the intent
  • Have Claude draft it, then rewrite the slop & add a first-hand tip
  • Add E-E-A-T: author bio, an original photo, a real "we tested this" note
  • Add internal links to the cluster (ratio post, best-moka-pot buyer's guide)
  • Check the Surfer/Frase content score & publish

Chapter Four · Technical SEO & Links

Fix the Plumbing, Earn a Link, Then Sit on Your Hands

Technical SEO is a checklist, not black magic — make sure Google can crawl, index, and load the page fast. Then the hardest SEO skill of all: patience. Rankings take weeks, and beginners who tweak the page every day never let Google settle.

📖 The story so far Publish day. Screaming Frog crawled the site and PageSpeed Insights flagged two easy wins. You'll fix the plumbing, submit the sitemap, earn a couple of honest links — and then, against every instinct, wait. Work through the checklist and watch the days unfold in the feed.

Technical + links checklist

  • Mobile + PageSpeed check — compress images, fix Core Web Vitals
  • Submit XML sitemap & request indexing in Search Console
  • Add schema markup (Article + FAQ) so rich results can show
  • Earn 2 ethical links: a guest post + a coffee-forum answer
  • Set a "do not touch the page for 3 weeks" reminder
  • Note the starting position & impressions in Search Console
