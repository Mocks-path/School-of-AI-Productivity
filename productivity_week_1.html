Chapter One · Monday

Excel in the AI Era — Meet the Tools, Pick the Right Helper

Rule one of AI Excel: there is no single "AI button." There are four very different helpers, each with a licence, a privacy trade-off, and a sweet spot. Today Maya matches the job to the right tool — before touching a single cell.

📖 The story so far Monday, 9:10 a.m. Maya opens sales_final_FINAL_v3.xlsx and her coffee goes cold. Product names like " Croissant " and "croissant" sit in the same column. A total in cell G2 reads #REF!. Her manager pings: "Clean weekly report every Friday 4 p.m., starting this week. Thanks!" Before she panics, she does the professional thing: she figures out which AI helper fits the job — and which she's even allowed to use with company data. Click each tool to inspect it, then commit.
🟩
Paid licence

Copilot in Excel

Microsoft 365 Copilot, built into Excel. Works on a formatted Table, edits your live file, writes formulas & charts. Powerful — but a paid add-on your workplace must enable.

🔎
Free · built-in

Analyze Data

The free "Analyze Data" button (Home tab) in any modern Excel. Suggests PivotTables, trends and charts with one click. No AI subscription, no data leaves your file.

💬
Privacy check first

ChatGPT / Claude

Brilliant at writing & explaining formulas from plain English. You can upload a file — but company sales data may not be allowed to leave the building. Read your policy.

🟦
If you're on Sheets

Gemini in Sheets

Google Workspace's AI, inside Google Sheets. Same idea as Copilot, different suite. Great — only if your bakery lives in Google, not Microsoft.

🕵️ Catch the AI's mistake — the sanity check

🎯 Checkpoint · The golden rule of automation

Maya's automation build checklist

  •  Duplicate the file  — test everything on a copy, never the original
  • Ask AI for an  Office Script  to trim + total + refresh the pivot
  • Read the script; run it once; compare outputs to yesterday's manual numbers
  • Add a  Power Automate  flow: run the script every Friday 3:30 p.m.
  • Auto-email the finished dashboard PDF to the manager
  • Write a 3-line "what this does" note so future-Maya trusts it
✅ The report now builds itself. Every Friday at 3:30, the script cleans, totals, refreshes and emails — Maya just double-checks and signs off. Zero code, written once.

Lesson 1

Excel in the AI Era

Four helpers, four trade-offs: Copilot (paid, in-file), free Analyze Data, ChatGPT/Claude (check privacy before uploading company data), Gemini in Sheets. Match the tool to the job. Chapter 1.

Lesson 2

AI Formulas & Functions

Describe the problem in plain English; AI returns TRIM, TEXTSPLIT, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, dynamic arrays. You read it, verify it, and fix #REF!/#N/A by understanding the cause. Chapter 2.

Lesson 3

AI Data Analysis & Insights

One-click PivotTables and charts, then "ask the data" in plain English — and always sanity-check a total against a sum you control. Chapter 3's caught error is the whole point.

Lesson 4

AI Automation & Reporting

Office Scripts + Power Automate turn a weekly chore into a scheduled, self-emailing dashboard — tested on a copy, never the original. Do it once, ship it forever. Chapter 4.

Rolling Pin Bakery is a teaching scenario. Real AI tools, licences and privacy rules change — always confirm what your workplace allows before uploading company data, and remember the golden rule this whole week was built on: AI is leverage, not truth. A human who reads the output ships the report.
