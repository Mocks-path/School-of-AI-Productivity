Is this recording consent-compliant?

Recording a meeting isn't just a button — it's a legal and trust question. Rules vary: some places allow one-party consent (one person on the call agreeing is enough), others require all-party consent (everyone must agree), and your employer's policy can be stricter than the law. This isn't legal advice — but you can reason about each scenario. Make the judgement call on all four, then check the plain-English verdict.
